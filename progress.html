How progress tracking works

Register once, then submit the Unit Progress and Reflection form after completing each of Units 1–7. Select the unit you completed and use the same email address as your registration.

Your personal learning checklist

Use this optional checklist on your device to remember which units you have worked through. It is not linked to your registration, does not verify completion, and may disappear if browser data is cleared.

Progress reports are self-reported and reviewed by the course instructor. Your browser-saved reflections and knowledge checks are not automatically transmitted to Google Sheets.

Steps

1. Finish the unit activities and knowledge check. 2. Prepare your reflection and classroom application. 3. Open the form below and select the unit. 4. Submit your response. 5. Return to the next unit.
