Why this course

Transportation is changing. Classroom examples should change with it.

Many teachers did not study autonomous vehicles, connected systems, electric mobility, intelligent transportation, or drones during their own education. This course develops enough foundational knowledge to discuss these ideas accurately and connect them with existing instruction.

1

Build understanding

Learn the underlying science, mathematics, computing, safety, energy, accessibility, and community ideas.

2

Experience the ideas

Work through short cases, investigations, simulations, diagrams, discussions, and low-cost alternatives.

3

Transfer to teaching

Adapt what you learn into a classroom-ready experience that fits your subject, learners, resources, and community.
