Teacher Discussion and Collaboration

Explore weekly discussion prompts, exchange ideas with colleagues, and seek instructor feedback.

Discussion activities: Use the seven unit prompts to prepare an evidence-based response and a constructive peer reply. The prompts are available now; online posting and live chat are not enabled on this website. You may send your response or questions to the instructor by email. Do not share identifiable student information.

Weekly discussion prompts

  1. Introduce a transportation challenge in your community and suggest a subject or grade connection.
  2. Share a prediction and observation from the Unit 2 energy-demand simulator.
  3. Compare two ways to teach sensors or data without specialized equipment.
  4. Exchange and critique a safe traffic-signal algorithm.
  5. Suggest how to make a pedestrian safety activity accessible to different learners.
  6. Discuss equity considerations in EV charging and access.
  7. Post your classroom-transfer plan and offer a peer one strength, one question, and one actionable suggestion.

How to participate

  1. Review the prompt connected to your unit.
  2. Prepare a short evidence-based response, identifying your grade and subject.
  3. Share your response with a colleague when a class discussion opportunity is arranged, or email your response to the instructor. Provide one constructive peer reply when peer exchange is available.
  4. Do not share learner names, school records, or confidential details.
