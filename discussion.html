Teacher Discussion and Collaboration

Explore weekly discussion prompts, exchange ideas with colleagues, and seek instructor feedback.

How collaboration works: The course offers seven discussion prompts and a structured peer-feedback process. This static website does not host public posts or live chat. To arrange a peer exchange, contact the instructor; participant contact information is not shared without permission. Instructor-scheduled live meetings are optional.

Weekly discussion prompts

  1. Introduce a transportation challenge in your community and suggest a subject or grade connection.
  2. Share a prediction and observation from the Unit 2 energy-demand simulator.
  3. Compare two ways to teach sensors or data without specialized equipment.
  4. Exchange and critique a safe traffic-signal algorithm.
  5. Suggest how to make a pedestrian safety activity accessible to different learners.
  6. Discuss equity considerations in EV charging and access.
  7. Post your classroom-transfer plan and offer a peer one strength, one question, and one actionable suggestion.

Peer discussion and collaboration pathway

For each unit, write a brief response to its prompt. When a peer exchange is available, review a colleague's response and provide constructive feedback. Participation may be asynchronous by instructor-coordinated email; the site does not display participant posts.

  1. Prepare: Choose the current unit prompt and explain your idea with a classroom example or evidence.
  2. Connect: Email the instructor to request a peer exchange or participate in a scheduled group activity. The instructor will coordinate participation with consent.
  3. Respond: Offer one strength, one question, and one actionable suggestion.
  4. Reflect: Record how the peer response could improve your lesson or classroom-transfer plan.
