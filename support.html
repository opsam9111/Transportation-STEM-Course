Technology and Course Support

Start with the simplest solution. If the problem remains, record what happened and contact Samuel or post a non-private question in the discussion forum.

Minimum Requirements

  • A modern browser on a phone, tablet, Chromebook, or computer
  • Internet access for course pages and submission
  • A way to create text, audio, video, slides, or visual responses
  • No required robot or electronics purchase

Quick Troubleshooting

  1. Refresh the page and retry the action.
  2. Check your internet connection and battery or power source.
  3. Try a different supported browser or device when available.
  4. Use the printable, simulated, low-bandwidth, or unplugged alternative.
  5. Record the page, device, browser, action, and error message.
  6. Email Samuel for private help or post a non-private question in the discussion forum.

Accessibility Controls

Use Text size, Contrast, and Read aloud in the top navigation. Keyboard users can press Tab to move through interactive controls. Video materials will include captions and transcripts; informative images will include alt text or a text description.

Equipment Support

Participants ready to implement selected activities may ask about available technology-support or equipment-lending opportunities. Availability is not guaranteed. Every major activity will include a low-cost, simulated, shared-resource, printable, or unplugged route whenever possible.

Emergency Access Plan

If electricity, internet access, employment responsibilities, or family needs interrupt participation, contact the instructor. Download materials when possible and use the offline alternative until access returns.
