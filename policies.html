Course Policies

These policies support clear expectations, flexibility, professional responsibility, privacy, and respectful participation.

Communication and Feedback

Messages receive a response within 24 hours on weekdays and 48 hours on weekends. Regular work normally receives feedback within five days; major projects may require seven days.

Late Work and Make-Up

Late work will normally be accepted without penalty when participants communicate promptly. Collaborative deadlines require early communication because classmates may depend on one another.

Participation and Professional Conduct

Participants should contribute respectfully, protect privacy, acknowledge different school and community conditions, and critique ideas without diminishing people.

Responsible AI

AI may support brainstorming, clarification, examples, language, practice, adaptation, visuals, accessibility review, and draft feedback. Participants must review, revise, disclose meaningful use, protect confidential information, and accept responsibility for final work.

Accessibility

Contact the instructor when a course format or activity creates a barrier. Reasonable alternatives will preserve the learning goal and common success criteria.
