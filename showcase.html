Week 15

Final Showcase and Reflection

Communicate, evaluate, and improve your classroom-ready transportation STEM learning experience.

ShowcasePurposePathwaysStepsSelf-reviewReflection

Showcase purpose

Demonstrate what you have learned by presenting either a classroom-ready transportation STEM lesson or a classroom implementation with non-identifying evidence.

Choose your pathway

Design pathway

Submit a complete lesson with objectives, content accuracy, activity sequence, standards, accessibility, low-cost alternative, assessment, and revision.

Implementation pathway

Submit your lesson, anonymized implementation evidence, interpretation of learner responses, and a justified next revision.

Step-by-step final task

  1. Choose a unit concept and define the target learners and context.
  2. Explain the transportation STEM concept accurately and show its relevance to a community concern.
  3. Present a measurable objective, a subject connection, and a verified standards connection.
  4. Demonstrate the learning sequence and explain what students will do at each step.
  5. Show a low-cost or unplugged alternative and two accessibility supports.
  6. Provide the assessment criteria and example evidence of learning.
  7. Describe feedback received and identify one specific revision.
  8. Present in an accessible written, visual, narrated, or video format; include captions or transcripts where relevant.

Self-review rubric

Check each element before submitting through your instructor's designated course space.
