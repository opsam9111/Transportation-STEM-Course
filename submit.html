Final Artifact Submission

Week 15: Submit your classroom-ready learning experience or implementation evidence.

Choose one pathway

Design pathway: A complete lesson with learning objectives, subject and grade connection, instructional sequence, verified standards, accessibility supports, low-cost alternative, assessment, and a justified revision.

Implementation pathway: A lesson plus non-identifying implementation evidence, interpretation of learner learning, and a proposed revision.

Before you upload

  1. Review the final showcase rubric.
  2. Choose an accessible format: PDF, DOCX, PPTX, or a link to an accessible resource, as permitted by the instructor's form.
  3. Remove student names, faces, and identifying details from evidence.
  4. Include your reflection and explain how feedback informed one revision.
  5. Open the secure submission form and follow its instructions.
  6. Save the form's confirmation message or receipt for your records.

Upload final artifact (not yet available)

Final project submissions are collected through the linked Google Form. You may need to sign in to a Google account to upload a file. Do not include identifiable student information.

Feedback and completion

The instructor reviews submitted work using the published rubric and maintains completion records in the external course tracker. This website does not automatically award a certificate or record a grade.
