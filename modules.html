Course Modules

Each two-week unit requires approximately four hours. Follow Learn, Experience, Adapt, Apply, and Reflect.

How to move through the course

Start with orientation and registration. For each unit, complete the investigation, knowledge check and reflection, then submit the Unit Progress form before continuing. Use the Progress page for an optional personal checklist; the Google Forms response is the official self-reported record.

New to the course? Review Orientation → Complete Teacher Registration → Begin Unit 1. After each unit, use Record Unit Progress. Registration is needed for instructor-managed completion tracking, but browsing remains open.
