Stage 7 · Course evaluation

Course Readiness and Learning Evidence

This page explains how teachers demonstrate learning and how the course is evaluated. It does not replace a learning-management system or claim that live testing is complete.

Learning evidence across the semester

Learn and Experience

Complete unit readings, guided media, models, investigations, and knowledge checks. Record observations, calculations, or design decisions.

Adapt and Apply

Identify a grade, subject, local problem, learning objective, standards connection, accessibility adjustment, and low-cost alternative.

Reflect and Improve

Use feedback and evidence to explain what worked, what remains uncertain, and what you would change.

Final project evidence checklist

Use the Final Showcase and Resources pages for submission expectations and the rubric.

  1. State the grade, subject, context, and transportation challenge.
  2. Specify measurable learning objectives and appropriate standards connections.
  3. Explain accurate transportation STEM content and the learning sequence.
  4. Provide materials, step-by-step instructions, and an unplugged or low-cost option.
  5. Identify accessibility and inclusive participation strategies.
  6. Describe how learning will be assessed, and include an evidence-based revision.

Evaluation and quality assurance

The course should be checked for content accuracy, objective–activity–assessment alignment, interaction, accessibility, and reliable navigation before being presented as fully tested.

  1. Navigate every page using keyboard alone, including menus and quizzes.
  2. Test all three Unit 2 sliders at minimum and maximum positions; check the displayed index stays between 0 and 100.
  3. Test Unit 3 color selections and ambient-light controls.
  4. Submit each knowledge check with correct and incorrect responses.
  5. Verify all video URLs, captions, and transcript or text alternatives.
  6. Check narrow-screen layouts and text-size and contrast preferences.
  7. Confirm where participant submissions, instructor feedback, and grades are handled.

Standards caution: NGSS connections are suggested learning links. Confirm the specific performance expectation and actual assessment evidence. Verify Alabama subject-specific standards from the official 2023 Course of Study before claiming exact alignment.

Data note: Some reflections and checklists are saved only in the current browser/device. They are not submitted to an instructor or synchronized across devices. Download your work and submit it using the course's designated channel.
